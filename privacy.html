# Privacy Policy This application is a personal OAuth client used with rclone to access my own Google Drive account. No personal data is collected, stored, shared, sold, or processed by any third party through this application. Google account authorization is handled through Google OAuth. Access tokens and configuration data are stored locally by rclone on my own device. The application only requests Google Drive access required for the intended rclone functionality. No data obtained through Google APIs is transferred to external servers or used for advertising, analytics, or profiling.
